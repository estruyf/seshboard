Switchboard has two searches: **Search conversations** looks through your prompts and Claude's replies in every session, and **Find in conversation** highlights words in the session you are reading.

<Screenshot name="search" alt="Search conversations: results grouped by session, each line marked You or Claude, with the matching words highlighted" eager />

## Open it

- **Search conversations:** press <Kbd shortcut="search" />, or choose *Search conversations* in the command palette.
- **Find in conversation:** press <Kbd shortcut="find" /> in a session.

## Search every conversation

<Steps>

1. Press <Kbd shortcut="search" /> and type what you are looking for.
2. Results are grouped by session, with the session's title, project and age. Each match is marked **You** (your prompt) or **Claude** (a reply), with the matching words highlighted. A session with more matches says "+N more". Matching memories show first, in their own group above the conversations; *Search memory…* in the command palette searches memory only. See [Search memory](/memory/links-and-search/#search-memory).
3. Use <Kbd keys="arrowup|arrowdown" /> to move through the matches, and <Kbd keys="enter" /> (or a click) to open one. Switchboard opens the session and jumps straight to that message.

</Steps>

Esc closes the search. When Switchboard has just started, it may still be reading your sessions: the search says so ("Indexing 120 of 300 sessions") and more results fill in as it goes.

The search covers the sessions the sidebar lists. With **Show sessions from other apps** off, it searches only the sessions you started or continued in Switchboard.

<Aside type="tip">
The **Search sessions** field at the top of the sidebar is different: it filters the list by title, project or branch. See [The sidebar](/sessions/sidebar/).
</Aside>

## Find in a conversation

<Screenshot name="find" alt="Find in conversation open over a session, with every match highlighted and the count of matches next to the field" />

Press <Kbd shortcut="find" /> in a session and type. Every match in the conversation is highlighted, and the bar says where you are ("2 of 7", or "No results").

- <Kbd shortcut="find.next" /> goes to the next match.
- <Kbd shortcut="find.previous" /> goes to the previous match.
- Esc, or the **×**, closes Find.

## Settings

**Show sessions from other apps** in [Settings, Sidebar](/settings/#sidebar) decides which sessions Search covers. Find has no settings.
