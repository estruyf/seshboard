Every session has a narrow column of buttons at its right edge: the rail. It holds the session's tools, such as Changes, the terminal and the browser, and the apps you pin to it, such as your editor. A badge on a button says what is in it, so you see at a glance that 3 files changed or 2 agents run, without opening anything.

Each pane in [split view](/sessions/split-view/) and each session in [its own window](/sessions/own-window/) has its own rail. Home, Upcoming, Activity, New session and the other views have none.

## What's on it

From top to bottom, in groups with a line between them:

| Group | Buttons |
|---|---|
| What the session made | [Files](/sessions/files/), [Changes](/terminal-changes-git/changes/), [Terminal](/terminal-changes-git/terminal/) and [Browser](/browser/in-a-session/) |
| Questions about it | [Ask](/sessions/ask/), [Agents](/sessions/agents/), [Commands](/sessions/commands/) and **Note** |
| [Mods](/sessions/mods/) | A letter tile for each pane a mod draws |
| Apps | The apps you pinned, each with its own icon. See [Apps on the rail](#apps-on-the-rail). |

**Tools panel** in the [title bar](/sessions/rail/#the-title-bar) shows or hides the right side: **Hide panel** closes whatever it shows, and **Show panel** opens it with a list of the tools to open there.

A tool shows only when it applies to the session, so the rail never has greyed-out buttons:

- **Files** needs the session's folder on this computer, and **Changes** a git repository.
- **Browser** shows while a dev server runs in the session, or always with [the browser](/browser/overview/) on. With the browser off it is the [Web preview](/sessions/web-preview/), called **Web**.
- **Agents** shows once Claude started an agent, **Commands** once it ran a command in the background, and **Note** once the session has a [note](/sessions/sidebar/#whats-left-a-note).

Point at a button for its name, what is in it and its key, such as "Changes · 3 files". The badges count the changed files, the terminals and agents and commands that run, and the session's browser tabs. A dot on **Ask** means an answer is ready, and a dot on **Browser** that a server is running to show.

## Open a tool

Click a button to open its panel on the right of the conversation. Several panels can be open at once, and the rail shows which:

- The one in front has a filled button with a mark on its left edge. Click it to close it.
- One open behind another has a small dot under its icon. Click it to bring it to the front.
- Click a closed one to open it in front.

The keys do the same: <Kbd shortcut="files.toggle" /> for Files, <Kbd shortcut="changes.toggle" /> for Changes, <Kbd shortcut="terminal.toggle" /> for the terminal, <Kbd shortcut="web.toggle" /> for the browser, <Kbd shortcut="agents.toggle" /> for Agents and <Kbd shortcut="commands.toggle" /> for Commands. The terminal keeps its place: docked below the conversation, its button shows and hides it there.

In a narrow session, a panel opens over the conversation instead of beside it.

With the keyboard, <Kbd keys="arrowup|arrowdown" /> move between the buttons on the rail.

## Show or hide tools

Right-click the rail (or press <Kbd shortcut="context-menu" /> on one of its buttons) for its menu:

- **Tools**: check or uncheck **Files**, **Changes**, **Terminal**, **Browser**, **Ask**, **Agents**, **Commands**, **Note** and **Mods**. A tool you hide keeps its key and its command in the palette.
- **Open in**: the apps to pin. See below.
- **Panels at half width**: every panel on the right takes half of the session. Drag a panel's edge to give it its own width again.
- **Reset** shows every tool again, unpins the apps and puts the buttons back in their first order.

## Apps on the rail

Under **Open in** in the rail's menu, check an app, such as VS Code, <Os mac="Finder" windows="File Explorer" />, a terminal or **GitHub**. It shows on the rail with its own icon, and a click opens the session's folder in it. Your default editor stays as it was.

**GitHub** opens the folder's page on GitHub. It shows only where the folder has a GitHub remote. With the browser on, it opens in Seshboard's browser.

The **Open in** menus elsewhere show each app's own icon too.

## Move a button

Drag a tool or an app to another place on the rail, or press <Kbd shortcut="rail.move" /> on it. A button moves only within its group: the tools of one group among themselves, the apps among the apps. A mod's tile doesn't move. Seshboard keeps the order for every session.
