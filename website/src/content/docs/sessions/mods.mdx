Mods are Claude Code plugins that draw UI: a band above the message box, a pane next to the conversation, a status in the message box, a toast, or their own view of part of the session, such as a tool call. Claude Code loads and runs them from your enabled plugins. Switchboard only draws what they return.

<Screenshot name="mods" alt="A session with two mods. Above the message box, a band from agent-watch says 3 of 3 agents running, with Show agents and Raise limit. On the right, the Agents pane from agent-watch lists three running agents, each with Stop. The header shows 2 mods" eager />

Everything a mod draws sits in a frame with a puzzle icon and the mod's name, so you always know where it comes from. A mod can't draw its own header, and it can't look like Switchboard's own cards, such as a permission prompt.

Mods need a Claude Code version with mod support. A session on an older version works as before, without them.

## Where mods show

- **Bands** sit above the message box. Switchboard's own permission and question cards always come first, then the mods' bands, then [Up next](/sessions/conversation/#up-next). A band is at most 160 pixels tall and scrolls inside.
- **Panes** open on the right, as tabs next to **Changes** and **Ask**, each with the pane's title. A line under the tabs says which mod draws it, such as *From agent-watch · pane*. Click **×** to close a pane. Some mods let <Kbd keys="escape" /> close their pane too; the keyboard then goes back to the message box.
- **Statuses** are chips in the message box, before **Send**: the mod's name and its text. Two fit at most. The rest go into a **+N** chip that lists them all. When the box gets narrow, the statuses fold into **+N** first, before anything else moves.
- **Toasts** show in the corner like Switchboard's own, with the mod's name on a small line above the text.
- **Replaced views**: a mod can draw its own view of a tool call, a message or the working line. The frame says what it replaces, such as *Drawn by agent-watch · replaces ToolUse*. **Show the built-in view** switches that one item back to Switchboard's view, for this session.

Buttons, fields and lists in a mod's drawing work with the keyboard and screen readers like the rest of the app. Links open in your browser, and only `http` and `https` links open at all. Nothing a mod draws can run code or load anything by itself.

Mods use the colours of your [theme](/themes/built-in/), so they look the same as the rest of the app in light and dark mode.

## The mods in a session

The puzzle chip in the session's header, such as **2 mods**, lists the mods in the session and what each one draws: band, pane, status, toasts, or what it replaces. Each has a **Show its UI** switch, which hides or shows that mod's UI in Switchboard.

<Screenshot name="mods-popover" alt="The Mods in this session popover: agent-watch draws a band, a pane and a status, pr-status draws a status. Each has a Show its UI switch. At the bottom, Settings › Mods and Logs" />

**Settings › Mods** opens the settings. **Logs** opens Settings › Diagnostics, where **Mod logs** shows what mods wrote to their log. Mod logs never show in the conversation.

## Prompt checks

Some mods check your prompts before Claude reads them. Then a green **checked** chip with a shield shows in the message box. The checking happens in Claude Code, so the shield stays when you turn mods off.

## Settings › Mods

<Screenshot name="settings-mods" alt="Settings, Mods: Show mods is on. Under it agent-watch and pr-status, each from the Mods profile with its folder, and each with its own switch. The note under them: A mod's switch only shows or hides its UI here" />

- **Show mods** is the main switch, on by default. Off, sessions don't attach their mods: no bands, panes, statuses, toasts or replaced views, and tool calls use the built-in view. The chip in the header goes away. Turning it off or on takes effect in open sessions at once, without a restart.
- Under it, each mod from your enabled plugins, per [Claude profile](/settings/claude-profiles/), with where it comes from: a plugin folder, a marketplace, or *Managed by your organization*. Each has a switch that shows or hides its UI here. With **Show mods** off, these switches are greyed out and keep their state for when you turn it back on.

<Aside>
A mod's switch only shows or hides its UI in Switchboard. To turn a mod off, disable its plugin in Claude Code.
</Aside>

When the session in front runs a Claude Code without mod support, the page says *Needs Claude Code with mod support. This session's version doesn't draw mods.*

## Other devices

Your phone and other computers don't show bands, panes or replaced views from mods.
