Pin a session you keep coming back to, and it stays in its own **Pinned** section near the top of the sidebar. A pinned session is never archived on its own, however long it stays quiet.

## Open it

The **Pinned** section is in the [sidebar](/sessions/sidebar/), below Needs you, Working and the Queue. In the minimal rail it is the group with the pin.

## Pin a session

- Right-click a session in the sidebar (or press <Kbd shortcut="context-menu" /> on it) and choose **Pin**.
- With a session open, choose *Pin* in the command palette (<Kbd shortcut="palette.commands" first />).
- Select several sessions with <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.select" first /> and choose **Pin** in the bar at the bottom of the sidebar.

A pinned session shows a small pin next to its age. To unpin it, choose **Unpin** in the same places.

## How pinned sessions behave

- A pinned session that needs you or is working shows under **Needs you** or **Working**, at the top of that section. When it is done, it goes back to **Pinned**.
- Pinned sessions never move to Archived by themselves.
- Archiving a pinned session unpins it. See [Archived sessions](/sessions/archived/).
- Close the Pinned section from its header like any other section; the session you have open stays visible under it.

## Settings

Pinning has no settings. Pinned sessions are left out of a settings export unless you tick them in [Settings, Backup](/settings/#backup).
