Home is what the window shows when no session is open. It uses the whole window: a box to start new work, lanes for what needs you, what is working and what to review, and under them your projects and usage.

<Screenshot name="home" alt="Home: a greeting and a start box, the lanes Needs you, Working and To review, then the projects with Up next, and usage on the right" eager />

## Open it

- Press <Kbd shortcut="home" />.
- Click **Home** under the search field in the sidebar (the house button in the minimal sidebar).
- Choose *Home* in the command palette.
- Close the session you have open with the **×** in its header, or *Close session* in the command palette.

Seshboard also opens on Home, unless you choose otherwise in Settings.

## Start from the box

Type a project's name and what Claude should do, then press <Kbd keys="enter" />: New session opens with both filled in. Press <Kbd keys="mod+1..9" /> to pick one of your projects by its number.

## The lanes

Every card opens its session.

- <Pill tone="needs-you">Needs you</Pill> **Needs you:** a card per session that waits for you, with what it asks for ("Permission: Bash", "Question", "Plan to review").
- <Pill tone="working">Working</Pill> **Working:** a card per session Claude is working on, with what it is doing.
- **To review:** finished sessions you haven't read, newest first. A card opens its session with the [catch-up](/sessions/catch-up/), and the lane can start [going through them one by one](/sessions/catch-up/#go-through-your-sessions).

In a narrower window the lanes stack. When an empty Needs you doesn't fit, the line under the greeting says it instead.

## Under the lanes

- **Start in a project:** your projects as rows, in New session's order and with the same keys. Click one to open New session on that project.
- **Up next:** the one thing Upcoming has next, with when. Click it to open [Upcoming](/sessions/upcoming/) on it.
- **Unsent:** the messages you typed and didn't send, each with **Open**. See [Unsent messages](/sessions/unsent-drafts/).
- **Queue:** your first queued prompts, with **Start** on the ones that are ready. **See all in Upcoming** opens the whole queue. See [Queue](/new-session/queue/).
- **Schedules:** your first scheduled sessions, each with its rule, its last and next run, **Run now** and ⋯. **See all in Upcoming** opens every schedule, and **Schedule a session** opens New session with Start set to **Schedule…**. They run <Os mac="while Seshboard is running, also with its windows closed" windows="while a Seshboard window is open" />. See [Schedules](/new-session/schedules/).
- **Usage**, in a column on the right: each profile's plan usage. A profile with sessions today keeps its card, and quiet ones take one line each. Click the usage to see what used it in the [Usage view](/usage/).

### Before you have projects

Without projects, Home says *Add your first project* with an **Add a project** button. See [First launch](/getting-started/first-launch/).

## Settings

- [Settings, General](/settings/#general): *On startup* picks what Seshboard shows when it opens: **Home** (the default), **The last session** or **New session**. [Settings, Projects](/settings/#projects): *Project order* picks the order of your projects: **Recent** (the ones you used last) or **Your order** (the order you set in Projects).
- **Show sessions from other apps** in [Settings, Sidebar](/settings/#sidebar) adds sessions from the terminal, Claude desktop and your editor to Needs you and Working.
