Home is what the window shows when no session is open. It tells you in one sentence what needs you and what is working, and gives you a quick way to start in one of your projects.

<Screenshot name="home" alt="Home: a card for a session that needs you, one that is working, the queue, four project tiles under Start in a project, and the two Claude profiles" eager />

## Open it

- Press <Kbd shortcut="home" />.
- Click the house button at the top of the sidebar.
- Choose *Home* in the command palette.
- Close the session you have open with the **×** in its header, or *Close session* in the command palette.

Switchboard also opens on Home, unless you choose otherwise in Settings.

## What's on it

From top to bottom:

- **The summary**, such as "1 session needs you, 2 are working.", and the **New session** button (<Kbd shortcut="session.new" />).
- <Pill tone="needs-you">Needs you</Pill> **Needs you:** a card per session that waits for you, with its project and branch, what it asks for ("Permission: Bash", "Question", "Plan to review") and **Open**.
- <Pill tone="working">Working</Pill> **Working:** a card per session Claude is working on, with what it is doing. Click one to open it.
- **Unsent:** the messages you typed and didn't send, each with **Open**. See [Unsent messages](/sessions/unsent-drafts/).
- **Queue:** your queued prompts, with **Start** on the ones that are ready and ⋯ for the rest. See [Queue](/new-session/queue/).
- **Start in a project:** tiles for four of your projects, with the branch checked out and how many sessions are open there. Click one to open New session on that project.
- **Profiles** (or **Usage** with one profile): each Claude profile's plan usage and what its sessions are doing. Click the usage to see what used it in the [Usage view](/usage/).

Needs you and Working only show when there is something in them. With the window narrow, the columns stack.

### Before you have projects

Without projects, Home says *Add your first project* with an **Add a project** button. See [First launch](/getting-started/first-launch/).

## Settings

- [Settings, General](/settings/#general): *On startup* picks what Switchboard shows when it opens: **Home** (the default), **The last session** or **New session**. [Settings, Projects](/settings/#projects): *Project order* picks which projects get a tile: **Recent** (the ones you used last) or **Your order** (the order you set in Projects).
- **Show sessions from other apps** in [Settings, Sidebar](/settings/#sidebar) adds sessions from the terminal, Claude desktop and your editor to Needs you and Working.
