The sidebar lists your sessions across every project, grouped by what needs attention first and then by what is still yours to finish. It is where you see at a glance which sessions are working, which wait for you, and which you still have to look at.

<Screenshot name="sidebar" alt="The sidebar: Needs you and Working at the top, the Queue under them, then Pinned, To review and Open, with Done docked at the bottom" width={480} eager />

## Open it

The sidebar is always on the left of the window. Press <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.toggle" /> or click the sidebar button at the left of a view's header to collapse it or open it again. The command palette has *Toggle sidebar*, *Open sidebar*, *Minimize sidebar* and *Close sidebar*.

## What's in it

At the top, a row of icons opens the views. Point at one to see what it holds:

- **Home** opens [Home](/sessions/home/) (<Kbd shortcut="home" />). It has a dot while a session needs you.
- **Upcoming** opens [Upcoming](/sessions/upcoming/) (<Kbd shortcut="upcoming" />): your queue and scheduled runs. Its count is your queued items plus your active schedules, in green when a queued item is ready. The tooltip says what comes next, such as "Upcoming · 1 ready" or "Upcoming · next 08:00".
- **Activity** opens [Activity](/sessions/activity/) (<Kbd shortcut="activity" />): what you did per day. The tooltip says how many sessions you had today.
- **Browser** opens the [Browser view](/sessions/web-preview/) (<Kbd shortcut="browser.view" />), when the browser is on.

Under them:

- **Search sessions** filters the list by title, project or branch. To search inside conversations, use [Search](/sessions/search/) (<Kbd shortcut="search" />).
- The yellow **+** button starts a [new session](/new-session/form/) (<Kbd shortcut="session.new" />).
- **All projects** filters the list to one project. Its menu lists your projects with their session count, plus **Manage projects…** and **Add a project folder…**. The ⋯ next to a project (or → on it) opens its options: icon, rename, open the project's page, Worktrees, Branches and more.
- The **View** button beside it, on the same row, says how the list is grouped: **State** or **Project**. See [View](#view).

A session you ran a [pipeline](/sessions/pipelines/) from shows where the run is, such as *Validate · 3 of 4*, with the pipeline icon. Its steps sit under it, closed at first: open them with the arrow. A run you started from New session has a row of its own with the pipeline icon instead of a project icon.

At the bottom, the footer counts your sessions ("12 sessions · 2 running · 1 queued · 1 waiting"), shows the [focus limit](/focus-limit/) counter when it is on and the [unsent messages](/sessions/unsent-drafts/) count, and has the **Projects** and **Settings** buttons.

**N open** in the footer counts what is still yours to finish: To review, Open and reminders that came due. Click it to [go through them one by one](/sessions/catch-up/#go-through-your-sessions).

### Star on GitHub

Once you have a few sessions, a **Star on GitHub** button shows above the footer (a star at the bottom of the minimal rail). If the [GitHub CLI](https://cli.github.com/) is installed and signed in, a click stars Seshboard with that account, without opening the browser. Otherwise it opens Seshboard on GitHub, where you can star it.

The button shows only until you star Seshboard or click it. Click **×** (right-click the star in the rail and choose **Don't show again**) to hide it for good.

## View

The **View** button holds how the list looks:

- **Group by**: **State** (the default) puts sessions in the [sections](#sections) below, such as Needs you and Working. **Project** puts them under their project. See [Group by project](#group-by-project).
- **Sort**: **Recent** or **Quickest to unblock**. Recent lists sessions as below; Quickest to unblock puts what needs you or a review first, the quickest at the top. See [Catch-up](/sessions/catch-up/#quickest-to-unblock).
- **Show**: **Done** shows the [Done](#close-a-section) section. Off, Done is gone from the list, but a search still finds what is in it. **Compact rows** shows one line per session, as the Compact style in [Settings](#settings).

The command palette has *Group sessions by project*, *Group sessions by state* and *Compact rows*.

### Group by project

Grouped by project, each project has a header with its icon, its name and how many sessions it holds. Under it, its sessions: what needs you first, then what is working, its queued prompts, what to review, and what is open. The projects with a session that needs you come first, then the others in the order you picked under **Sort**. Done comes after the projects.

- Click a project's header, or its chevron, to fold it. A folded project keeps its header and one line that says what is in it, such as "1 needs you · 2 working". The session you have open stays visible under it.
- <Kbd keys="alt+click" /> on a header folds or unfolds every project. **Collapse all projects** in the View menu, or <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.fold-projects" />, does the same.
- Point at a header for **New session in** the project (**+**) and its menu (**⋯**). Right-click the header for the menu too.
- With the keyboard, <Kbd keys="arrowleft" /> folds a project and <Kbd keys="arrowright" /> unfolds it. <Kbd keys="enter" /> or <Kbd keys="space" /> on its header does either.

A project on [another computer](/other-computers/) has its own header, with the computer's chip.

Grouping by project works with **All projects**. With one project picked in the filter, the list is grouped by state, and the View button says so in a paler colour.

## Sections

When the list is grouped by state, it is split into sections. Empty sections don't show.

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Needs you** | Sessions waiting for a permission, an answer or a plan review. The second line says what it asks for, such as "Permission: Bash" or "Question". |
| **Working** | Sessions Claude is working on, the one you last sent a message to at the top. |
| **Queue** | Prompts waiting to start, right under Working. See [Queue](/new-session/queue/). |
| **Pinned** | Sessions you pinned. See [Pinned sessions](/sessions/pinned/). |
| **Reminders** | Sessions you asked to be [reminded about](/sessions/upcoming/#remind-me-later), once it is time. They stay here until you open them. |
| **To review** | Finished sessions you haven't read yet, and sessions that failed, newest first. |
| **Open** | Sessions you read but haven't marked done, newest first. |
| **Done** | Sessions you marked done, and sessions that were quiet for the days set in [Settings › Sessions](/settings/sessions/) (3 by default). See [Done](/sessions/archived/). |

A pinned session that needs you or is working shows in Needs you or Working, and goes back to Pinned when it is done. A session with an [unsent message](/sessions/unsent-drafts/) or a queued prompt stays listed however old it is, so it never slips into Done.

[Quick questions](/new-session/quick-questions/) are sessions like any other: they sit in the same sections, with their own icon and *Questions* where a project name would be.

Each row shows the project's icon, the title, its age, and a second line with the branch and project. A session with a [catch-up](/sessions/catch-up/) says how long it takes to review instead ("acme-store · ~2 min to review"), and a warning shows when Claude changed files outside the plan. The [state](/sessions/states/) shows as a coloured rail on the left edge, the colour of the age and a status icon. A pin marks a pinned session and a pen an unsent message.

### What's left: a note

Give a session one line, such as "push after review" or "waiting on Jan". Press <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.note" /> on its row or <Kbd shortcut="catchup.note" /> in its catch-up, or choose **Note…** in its menu or the command palette. The note shows under the title (in the tooltip with the compact style), and search finds it. A catch-up that needs something from you offers **Use as note**, which puts that there in one click. Done keeps the note but doesn't show it.

The note also shows in the session's header, after the branch. Click it to open the **Note** panel on the right, with the whole note: change it and click **Save** (<Kbd keys="enter" />), or **Clear** it. While the session has a note, **Note** is on the [tool rail](/sessions/tool-rail/) too. A row whose note has no line of its own, with compact rows or while the session needs you, shows a note icon instead, with the note in its tooltip.

### Loose ends

A session that left something behind has a small marker on its row: files not committed, commits not pushed, a pull request with failing checks, one closed without merging, or a queued prompt. Hover the marker to see what.

Such a session never goes to Done by itself after the days you set. You can still mark it done yourself, and the toast then says what's left, such as "3 files not committed". Seshboard looks when a turn ends, when you come back to the window, and every few minutes.

### Close a section

Every section but Needs you, Reminders and To review closes from its header: click it, or press <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.section-toggle" /> on it. <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.sections-all" /> on a header closes or opens every section at once. The chevron in each header points right while the section is closed and down while it is open.

A closed section keeps its count and says what matters inside, such as "2 unread" or, for the Queue, "1 ready". The session you have open stays visible under its closed section, with "+N hidden" under it. Going to a session in a closed section opens it again. While you search or filter by project, every section shows.

**Done** sits docked at the bottom of the sidebar while it is closed. Click it to open it: it slides up right under your other sessions. Its **⋯** has **Manage sessions…** and **Done settings…**, which open [Settings › Sessions](/settings/sessions/). To leave Done out of the list, uncheck **Done** in the [View](#view) menu.

Done also lists the sessions marked done on your [other computers](/other-computers/#done-on-another-computer), and the ones that went quiet there, with the computer's chip.

The session count at the bottom of the sidebar opens [Settings › Sessions](/settings/sessions/) too, with every session you have.

## Work with sessions

- **Open a session:** click it. <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.move" /> moves through the list.
- **Open it beside the current one:** <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.open-beside" />. See [Split view](/sessions/split-view/).
- **Move between sessions from anywhere:** <Kbd shortcut="session.next" /> and <Kbd shortcut="session.previous" /> go to the next and previous session in the sidebar's order, and <Kbd shortcut="session.next-needs-you" /> to the next one that needs you.
- **Go back and forward:** the back and forward buttons on your mouse return to where you were before, as in a browser: sessions, Home, New session, Settings pages, Projects and project pages. A session or project that is gone is skipped.
- **Rename:** press <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.rename" />, or choose **Rename…** in the session's menu.
- **Mark a finished session done:** middle-click it (<Kbd shortcut="sidebar.archive" />). Sessions that are working, need you or failed stay where they are.
- **Come back to it later:** press <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.remind" /> to [remind yourself](/sessions/upcoming/#remind-me-later).
- **Delete:** press <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.delete" />, or choose **Delete session…**. After you confirm with **Move to <Os mac="Trash" windows="Recycle Bin" />**, the conversation and any subagent transcripts go to the <Os mac="Trash" windows="Recycle Bin" />, so you can get them back.

### Quick actions on hover

Hover a session and buttons show at the end of its row, so you can tidy the list in one click:

- **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**, **Pin** or **Unpin**, and **Done** on a finished session.
- **Pin**, **Stop** and **Done** on a session that is working or needs you.
- **Reopen** on a session under Done.
- On a [pipeline](/sessions/pipelines/)'s row, the first button shows or hides its steps.

Each button does the same as its item in the session menu. A toast then says what it did, such as "Pinned", with **Undo**. <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.undo" /> in the sidebar undoes it too, while the toast is up.

**Done** on a session that is still working or waits for you asks first: **Stop and mark done** stops it, then marks it done. A session that runs in another app keeps running there after you mark it done.

The keys do the same on the focused session, or the one under the pointer, also with the buttons turned off:

- <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.quick-archive" /> marks the session done, or reopens it.
- <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.quick-read" /> marks it read or unread.
- <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.quick-pin" /> pins or unpins it.

Press <Kbd keys="tab" /> on a row to move into its buttons, and <Kbd keys="escape" /> to go back to the row. Turn the buttons off, or down to Done only, in [Settings](#settings).

### The session menu

Right-click a session, or press <Kbd shortcut="context-menu" /> on it, for:

- **Rename…**
- **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**
- **Pin** or **Unpin**
- **Done** (until new activity) or **Reopen**
- **Note…** and **Remind me…**
- **Open beside**, **Open in new window** and **New session linked to this one**
- **Open folder in editor**
- **Copy session ID**
- **Project**: the session's project, with its **Claude profile** (when you have more than one), **Project icon**, **Rename project…**, **Open folder in editor**, **Open project**, **Worktrees…**, **Branches…** and **Project settings…**
- **Memories**: the memories the session wrote, when it wrote any
- **Delete session…**

<Screenshot name="session-menu" alt="A session's menu in the sidebar with its Project submenu open: the project's name and folder, Claude profile and Project icon, then Rename project…, Open folder in editor, Open project, Worktrees…, Branches… and Project settings…" width={720} />

**Project** and **Memories** open a submenu. Point at them, or press <Kbd keys="arrowright" /> or <Kbd keys="enter" />; <Kbd keys="arrowleft" /> or <Kbd keys="escape" /> goes back.

### Select several sessions

<Kbd shortcut="sidebar.select" /> selects several sessions. Every row then shows a round checkbox, and a bar at the bottom of the sidebar holds the actions for the selection: **Done** (or **Reopen**), **Pin** (or **Unpin**) and **Delete…**.

- <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.select-group" /> or **Select all** on a section header selects every session in that section.
- <Kbd keys="space" /> ticks the focused row.
- <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.clear-selection" /> or **Clear** ends the selection.
- Right-click one of the selected sessions for the same actions, and **Remind me about** them.

## Resize and collapse

The sidebar has three states: **open**, a **minimal rail** of project icons, and **closed**.

- Drag the sidebar's right edge to make it wider or narrower. Double-click the edge to reset the width.
- Drag it further in and it snaps to the rail; further still, it hides. Drag the edge out again to open it at the width you had.
- With the edge focused, ← and → step through the same states.
- <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.toggle" /> switches between open and collapsed. Settings decides whether collapsed means the rail or hidden.

Seshboard remembers the width and the state.

<Screenshot name="sidebar-rail" alt="The sidebar collapsed to the minimal rail: one project icon per session, in the same groups and order" width={480} />

The **minimal rail** is 80px wide: one project icon per session, in the same groups and order as the open sidebar, with the same coloured rails. Hover an icon to see the session's title, project, branch and state. Click a group's letter to open the sidebar there. At the top, under Home, the calendar button opens [Upcoming](/sessions/upcoming/). Its badge shows the count, in green when a queued item is ready. The clock under it opens [Activity](/sessions/activity/).

With the sidebar **hidden**, the conversation takes the whole window. A <Pill tone="needs-you">needs you</Pill> pill in the header says when sessions wait for you; click it to open the one waiting longest. After a jump with <Kbd shortcut="session.next" /> or <Kbd shortcut="session.next-needs-you" />, a short label at the top of the window says where you landed.

<Aside type="tip">
<Kbd shortcut="palette.goto" /> lists your sessions in the same groups as the sidebar, so you can jump anywhere while it is collapsed.
</Aside>

## Settings

In [Settings, Sidebar](/settings/#sidebar):

- **Sidebar style:** **Large icons** (each project easy to spot), **Standard** (the default) or **Compact** (one line per session). The command palette has *Sidebar: Large icons*, *Sidebar: Standard* and *Sidebar: Compact*.
- **When collapsed:** what <Kbd shortcut="sidebar.toggle" /> and the sidebar button do: **Minimal rail** (the default) or **Hidden**.
- **Show sessions from other apps:** also list sessions from your terminal, Claude desktop and your editor. Off (the default) shows only sessions you started or continued in Seshboard.
- **Quick actions on hover:** show the [buttons on a row under the pointer](#quick-actions-on-hover) (on by default). **Show** picks **Done only** or **Read · Pin · Done** (the default). Working sessions show Stop too.
