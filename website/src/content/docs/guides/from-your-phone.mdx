Claude often works for minutes and then stops to ask before it runs a command. With Remote, the question comes to your phone and you answer it there. At the end of this guide your phone is paired, it gets a notification when a session needs you, and you can answer, send a prompt and start a session from it.

## Turn on Remote

Open Settings (<Kbd shortcut="settings" />) and choose **Remote**. Under **Connection**, click **Change…** to choose a tunnel, which gives your computer an address your phone can reach:

- **Cloudflare quick tunnel** needs `cloudflared` and no account. Its address changes each time it starts.
- **Tailscale** needs Tailscale on your computer and your phone. It is the most private, and its address stays the same.
- **ngrok** and **My own address** are there too.

<OnlyOn os="mac">
Install `cloudflared` with Homebrew: `brew install cloudflared`.
</OnlyOn>

<OnlyOn os="windows">
Install `cloudflared` with winget: `winget install Cloudflare.cloudflared`.
</OnlyOn>

Click **Turn on Remote** and wait until the card says **Running**. See [Remote](/remote-access/).

## Pair your phone

<Steps>

1. Click **Pair a device**. Switchboard shows a QR code that works once, for two minutes.
2. Scan it with your phone's camera. The remote app opens and asks for a name for the phone.
3. Tap **Pair**. Your phone and Switchboard each show a 6-digit code.
4. Check that both codes are the same, then click **Allow**.

</Steps>

Add it to your home screen to open it like an app. On an iPhone, pair from the Home Screen app rather than from Safari: [Remote](/remote-access/) explains how.

## Turn on notifications

After you pair, a card on the phone offers **Turn on**. Later, open **Notifications** in the **⋯** menu and turn **Needs you** and **Finished** on or off. Nothing comes while the app is open in front of you. On an iPhone, notifications only work from the Home Screen app.

At your desk, Switchboard's own notifications and sounds are set in [Notifications and sounds](/settings/notifications/).

## Answer a permission

What Claude asks shows as cards with a pink frame on the first screen, the oldest first. A command, an edit or a fetch has **Deny** and **Allow** right on the card. A question or a plan is a short row: tap it to answer in the session.

Inside a session, the question stays above the message box, with **Deny**, **Always allow** when it's offered, and **Allow**. To say no and tell Claude what to do instead, type it in the message box and send. If you already answered on your computer, the phone says **Already answered**. See [Permissions and questions](/permissions/) for what each answer does.

## Send a prompt

Open a session and type in the message box. While Claude works, your message waits in Up next. A finished session starts with its catch-up. **Changes** on it shows what changed, and **Commit** there commits it; nothing is pushed. Swipe a session right to mark it as read, or left to archive it.

## Start a session

Tap **New session** at the bottom of the screen. Pick a project from the tiles, then type what Claude should do. The chips under the box start from the project's defaults: model, effort, mode, and **This folder** or **New worktree**. Tap **Start session**. While a session works in that project, **Queue for later** puts the prompt in Switchboard's queue instead.

<Aside>
Your computer has to be on, with Switchboard running. Sleep stops Remote until the computer wakes up.
</Aside>

## More than one computer

Your phone can pair with several computers, such as a laptop and a desktop: tap the computer's name at the top, then **Pair another computer…**. The sessions of all your computers are then in one list, and each says which computer it runs on, such as **mystic**. A chip for each computer at the top shows its status and how many of its sessions need you. In **New session**, **Run on** picks the computer a session starts on. See [More than one computer](/remote-access/#more-than-one-computer).

Two computers can also pair with each other, so the sessions of one show in the sidebar of the other. See [Other computers](/other-computers/).

<Aside type="tip">
A Cloudflare quick tunnel gets a new address each time it starts. Your phone doesn't have to pair again: click **Reconnect a paired device** in Settings and scan that code with the phone. With Tailscale the address stays the same.
</Aside>
