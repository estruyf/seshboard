## LinkedIn

Switchboard 0.0.18 is out, and this one lets you leave your desk.

Remote. Your Claude Code sessions, in your phone's browser. Turn it on in Settings, pick a tunnel (a Cloudflare quick tunnel, Tailscale, ngrok or your own address), scan the QR code, and allow the phone on your computer. From then on you can:

• see which sessions need you, and allow or deny what Claude asks right from the list
• answer Claude's questions and plans, and read along as it works
• look at the changes and commit them (it never pushes)
• start a new session in one of your projects, or queue one for later
• get a notification when a session needs you or finishes

Only phones you pair can connect, everything is encrypted from your phone to your computer so the tunnel can't read it, and Revoke cuts a phone off at once.

Also new on the desktop:

• Catch-up: when a session finishes, a short briefing says what it needs from you next (commit, reply or review), whether the tests passed, how much changed and whether Claude followed its plan. One key for each action.
• Ask: ask Claude about a session (what changed, why this approach) while it keeps working, without anything landing in the conversation.
• An Agents panel next to the conversation, to follow the agents Claude starts.
• Why? on a permission card: why Claude needs it and what saying yes does.
• Sort the sidebar by Quickest to unblock.

Switchboard is free and open source, for macOS and Windows: https://switchboard.struyfconsulting.com/
