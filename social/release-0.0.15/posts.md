## LinkedIn

Switchboard 0.0.15 is out, with my two favourite features so far.

Auto model. Not every prompt needs Opus. Pick Auto in the model menu and Haiku reads your prompt, then picks Haiku, Sonnet or Opus for it. The session opens right away, so you can start the next one while it decides. It tells you why it picked a model, and Run again with Opus or Use Opus from the next message undo it. Haiku only sees your prompt's text, never your files or memory, and when a session grows, Auto can suggest a stronger model, but never switches by itself.

Usage view. Click a usage bar and see where your plan went:

• Limits: what's left of each window, when it comes back, your % used over time, and which sessions used the current 5-hour window
• Tokens: where the tokens went per session, project, model and day, with the cache hit rate and fast mode turns
• The current window, 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days

Also new: Quote in reply, lists and code in the message box, back and forward with your mouse, all your sessions in one table where you can unarchive or clean up a whole month at once, and an option to move old archived sessions to the Trash by itself.

Switchboard is free and open source, for macOS and Windows: https://switchboard.struyfconsulting.com/
