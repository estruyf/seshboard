## LinkedIn

Switchboard now runs on Linux. 🐧

This is the first 1.1.0 beta, running in Omarchy: all my Claude Code sessions in one window.

After macOS and Windows, Linux felt like the natural next step. It fits Switchboard well: people who live in a terminal and a tiling window manager are often the ones running several Claude Code sessions at once.

What's in the beta for Linux:

• An AppImage, a .deb for Debian and Ubuntu, and an .rpm for Fedora, RHEL and openSUSE, for x64 and Arm64.
• Updates that install themselves: the AppImage in place, the .deb and .rpm through your package manager.
• Open in finds your file manager and terminals such as Ghostty, kitty, Alacritty and Konsole, and notification sounds follow your desktop's sound theme.

It's a beta, so I'd really like to hear how it runs on your distro and desktop. Issues and ideas are welcome on GitHub.

Download the beta: https://github.com/estruyf/switchboard/releases/tag/v1.1.0-beta.1
