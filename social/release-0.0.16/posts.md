## LinkedIn

Switchboard 0.0.16 is out, and it's all about not waiting for Claude.

When Claude is busy on a long task, you often think of the next thing, or want to know what it's doing. Until now that meant waiting, or stopping it. Not anymore.

• Side questions. Type /btw and a question to ask Claude about the session. It works while Claude is busy, Claude isn't stopped, and nothing is added to the conversation. Switchboard keeps your last 50 per session.
• Up next. Messages you send while Claude works wait above the message box and go one at a time when the turn ends. Edit, reorder or remove them, or press Send now.
• Steer. Prefer Claude to read your message at its next step, without stopping? Pick Steer in Settings, or press Option Enter.
• Create pull requests in Switchboard. Title, description from your repository's template, base branch and Draft. Switchboard pushes the branch and creates it with the GitHub CLI, no browser needed. After that, the git button shows View PR.

Also new: search in Settings, defaults per Claude profile, a permission mode for new sessions, a sound you can tell apart for each moment, switching branches from the Branches tab, and adding files from anywhere on your disk.

Switchboard is free and open source, for macOS and Windows: https://switchboard.struyfconsulting.com/
