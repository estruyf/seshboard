## LinkedIn

If you run more than one Claude Code session at a time, you know the feeling: five terminal tabs, and somewhere in one of them Claude has been waiting for your permission for twenty minutes.

That's why I built Switchboard, a free, open-source desktop app for Mac and Windows that puts all your Claude Code sessions in one window. It runs on the Claude Code you already have, with your own login, settings, skills and MCP servers. Nothing new to sign in to.

What it gives you:

• Every session in one list, the ones in your terminal too, sorted by what needs you first.
• Several Claude accounts side by side, like a personal plan and a work one, with usage and limits per account.
• Switchboard Remote: allow, deny and answer Claude from your phone, encrypted from your phone to your computer.
• A catch-up when a session finishes: what it needs from you next, whether the tests passed, how much changed, and whether Claude followed its plan.
• A worktree per task in one click, and an overview that tells you which old worktrees and branches are safe to remove.
• A usage view that shows where your plan went, and an Auto model that picks Haiku, Sonnet or Opus for each prompt.
